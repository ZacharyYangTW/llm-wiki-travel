Inca Trail to Machu Picchu                                                   <br>             <br>         Huayllabamba Campsite:         <br> Huayllabamba is a small Andean village where the community, in agreement with the Peruvian government provides their terraces to tour operators in order to use them as campsites. Usually most of the sites have bathrooms but do not expect there to be one like at home because they are just stand up bathrooms with wash basins. Huayllabamba campsite is a nice spot but be prepared to be awoken by roosters very early in the morning.
