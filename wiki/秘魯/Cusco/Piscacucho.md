Inca Trail to Machu Picchu                                                   <br>             <br>         Piscacucho Checkpoint:         <br> Piscacucho is the first checkpoint for all trekkers starting the Inca Trail. SERNANP (Servicio Nacional de Áreas Naturales Protegidas por el Estado) is the government entity in charge of the National Park System. The function of this control is to make sure that all the groups have their documentation in order and the appropriate equipment to guarantee a safe journey. First aid kits will be checked and bags of the porters will be weight so they don’t exceed the weight limit restriction. Agents will also verify you came with an authorized tourism agency and that you have your permits. Don’t forget to carry your passport with you, otherwise you won’t be allowed to pass. In this first checkpoint at Piscacucho you can ask the agents to stamp your passport for proof of your adventure!
