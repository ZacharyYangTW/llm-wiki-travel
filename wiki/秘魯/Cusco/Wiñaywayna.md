Inca Trail to Machu Picchu                                                   <br>             <br>         Wiñaywayna Campsite:         <br> This is the third and last campsite of the Inca Trail. This campsite has some facilities. You will find bathrooms, hot showers, electricity and food including cold beers. However keep in mind that there will probably be lot of people trying to use the facilities so you will have form a que and be patient until it is your turn.
